News & Tools

open-daams — Open-Source Reference Implementation of an EHDS Data Access Application Management System
A community-built (non-official) reference implementation of a DAAMS for a fictional "HDAB-NL", based on TEHDAS2 deliverable D6.4; includes append-only audit trails covering application/permit/SPE-provisioning state transitions — a concrete, inspectable model of SPE provisioning workflow that HDABs could reference or fork.
📅 2026 · GitHub · Not affiliated with the European Commission, TEHDAS2 or any national HDAB · github.com/mdevalk/open-daams
TEHDAS2 Wave-3 Consultation Closed; Joint Action Runs to December 2026
The third and final TEHDAS2 public consultation (data enrichment, third-country collaboration, informing citizens) closed at end of June 2026. TEHDAS2 continues finalising operational guidance and technical specifications for EHDS secondary use through its close-out in December 2026 — outputs (dataset-description guidelines, access-body operating guidance) are being published on a rolling basis.
📅 Ongoing through Dec 2026 · tehdas.eu/results
HealthData@EU Pilot — Ongoing Cross-Border Secondary-Use Infrastructure
EU4Health-co-funded pilot connecting data platforms and prospective HDABs across 8 Member States, providing a working model of the discovery → application → secure-processing → publication user journey that the EHDS regulation now formalises.
