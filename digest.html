News & Tools

EHDS2 Central Platform Release 3 Goes Open Source, with eDelivery Integration
Source code and documentation for the HealthData@EU Central Platform (Release 3) are now published on GitHub; the central broker works on metadata only, while execution stays local within each node's SPE and only de-identified aggregates leave the node — a concrete reference for federated SPE architecture.
📅 2026 · EC Digital Building Blocks
openEHDS — Community Open-Source Initiative for EHDS Building Blocks
A community-driven GitHub organisation building transparent, auditable, reusable open-source components aligned with EHDS legal, governance and operational requirements, aimed at healthcare providers, public authorities, researchers and vendors preparing to operate within the EHDS.
📅 Ongoing · github.com/openEHDS
TEHDAS2 Publishes Technical Specifications for Secure Processing Environments
TEHDAS2 (running to December 2026, 29 countries) has finalised technical specifications on how SPEs should be designed, operated and governed under EHDS, plus an operational guideline for researchers on secure use of health data inside an SPE.
📅 2026 · tehdas.eu
AI Act Regulatory Sandbox Deadline Pushed to August 2027
Under the Digital Omnibus provisional agreement (May 2026), the deadline for Member States to have an operational AI Act regulatory sandbox slipped a year, from 2 August 2026 to 2 August 2027 — only one Member State was operational at the time of the extension. An EU-level sandbox run by the AI Office is also being discussed as a complementary layer. Not a funding call, but relevant to HDA's AI-governance advisory work; the >€220M Testing & Experimentation Facilities envelope from the last digest is unchanged.
