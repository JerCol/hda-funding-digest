🆕 TEHDAS2 Publishes First Practical Guideline: "How to Use Data in a Secure Processing Environment (SPE)"
TEHDAS2 released the first in a series of practical tools to help data users navigate EHDS Secure Processing Environments. The guideline defines SPE workflows, access protocols, and responsibilities for researchers, policy makers, and innovators — the foundational document for any HDAB configuring or procuring an SPE under EHDS.
📅 2026 · tehdas.eu
🆕 Digital Omnibus Provisional Agreement — High-Risk AI Compliance Deadline Extended to December 2027
On 7 May 2026, EU co-legislators reached provisional agreement on the Digital Omnibus: the Annex III high-risk AI system compliance deadline (originally 2 August 2026) is extended to 2 December 2027. This directly affects health AI systems classified as high-risk under EHDS/AI Act dual governance. Timeline relief, but obligations remain; GPAI rules still apply from August 2026.
📅 7 May 2026 · artificialintelligenceact.eu · Latham & Watkins briefing
