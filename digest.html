New Research: PETs for EHDS Secondary Use — Framework & Research Agenda
A peer-reviewed paper published in PMC sets out the scene for privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) in EHDS secondary use and proposes a research agenda — covering federated learning, synthetic data, homomorphic encryption, and secure multi-party computation as candidate SPE building blocks.

Gatekeeper Platform: Kubernetes-Based SPE for EHDS
The Gatekeeper project (QMUL/EU consortium) published its SPE architecture for EHDS, using Kubernetes for tenant-specific service deployment with HPC, storage and horizontal scaling — a concrete open reference architecture for cloud-native EHDS Secure Processing Environments.
📅 2025–2026 · QMUL / EU pilot · PDF — QMRO
EHDS Regulation Now in Application — Secondary Use Framework Active from March 2026
The EHDS Regulation applies from 26 March 2026. Health Data Access Bodies must be designated by Member States by 26 March 2027. Secondary use rules for most data categories enter full application in March 2029 — but HDAB setup, SPE provisioning, and permit workflows must begin now.
📅 March 2026 · EC Health · health.ec.europa.eu — Projects supporting EHDS
HealthData@EU Pilot — Outcomes Published, Infrastructure Recommendations Available
The HealthData@EU Pilot (Oct 2022 – Dec 2024), led by France's Health Data Hub with 16 EU partners and 12 countries, published its final outcomes: network infrastructure recommendations, SPE data access workflows, HDAB governance templates, and reusable open-source connection components for national nodes.
📅 2024–2026 · Health Data Hub / EC · health-data-hub.fr · EC EHDS projects page
