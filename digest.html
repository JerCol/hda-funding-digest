TEHDAS2 Publishes D7.2 — Guideline for HDABs on Data Minimisation, Pseudonymisation, Anonymisation & Synthetic Data
New practical guidance for Health Data Access Bodies on how to reduce identifiability of datasets released for secondary use — directly informs SPE data-preparation workflows and dataset quality-label criteria.

Stakeholder Coalition Issues Joint Statement on TEHDAS2 EHDS Guidelines
Research, patient and industry organisations jointly welcomed the TEHDAS2 guidelines but stressed that semantic and structural interoperability must be treated as a foundational precondition — without it, data will be accessible but not reliably combinable across borders.
📅 29 June 2026 · tehdas.eu
Simpl-Open: EU's Open-Source Middleware for Data Space Federations Extends Toward EHDS
The Commission's free, open-source "Simpl" middleware for cloud-to-edge data-space federation (interoperability, portability, GDPR/AI Act/Data Act alignment) is being explored as a shared foundation for sector data spaces including EHDS, with eDelivery integration work ongoing.
