EHDS Regulation Now Formally Applicable; Secondary-Use Provisions Still on a Later Timeline
The EHDS Regulation has applied since 26 March 2026, but most secondary-use (EHDS2) provisions phase in only from March 2029, with Member-State implementation work running 2027–2035 — SPE deployment guidance is still being developed rather than finalised.
📅 2026 · PMC — Secondary use under EHDS: research agenda on PETs
HealthData@EU Central Platform Reaches Release 6 (of 9), Open Source
The Commission's open-source HealthData@EU Central Platform (hosting the EU-wide Dataset Catalogue, source at code.europa.eu) is now at Release 6 of a planned 9, with eDelivery integration for secure cross-border transmission — a direct building block for federated/SPE-adjacent architectures.
