News & Tools

TEHDAS2 Publishes New Technical Specifications for Secure Processing Environments
TEHDAS2 (29-country EU Joint Action, coordinated by Sitra) released specifications defining how SPEs required under EHDS must be designed, operated, and governed — giving Health Data Access Bodies a common technical/functional/security baseline. Part of a planned set of 20 EHDS guidelines and specifications.
📅 June 2026 · tehdas.eu
HealthData@EU Central Platform — Open Source, Release 7 Shipped
The EC's open-source HealthData@EU Central Platform (published on code.europa.eu, built on HealthDCAT-AP and Piveau-Hub components) shipped Release 7 in May 2026, connecting national health data catalogues and HDABs to the EU-level secondary-use infrastructure. Release 8 is expected in September 2026.
📅 Updated May 2026 · Platform: code.europa.eu · EC Newsroom
openEHDS — New Open-Source Components Initiative for EHDS
A GitHub organisation ("openEHDS") has emerged aiming to provide open-source components for the European Health Data Space. Still small (early-stage, limited repos/activity) — worth watching as a possible community complement to the Commission's own code.europa.eu releases, but not yet a mature reference implementation.
📅 Ongoing · github.com/openEHDS
